# Neocloud Lambda secures $1B in debt to buy more chips

**Source**: TechCrunch AI | **Published**: Fri, 28 Aug 2026 20:24:11 +0000 | **Topics**: ai_industry, ai_compute_energy

## Summary

- 연이은 차입으로 AI 붐의 조달 비용 부각

## Why This Matters
